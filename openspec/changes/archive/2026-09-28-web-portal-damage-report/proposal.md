## Why

The portal is where a tenant already goes: it is the one address they have for
the place they live. Reporting a leak there costs them nothing to learn.

The alternative is what happens now — a text message to whoever they have the
number of, which leaves no record of what was said or when somebody promised to
come.

## What Changes

- The portal gains a way to report something broken: what it is, in their own
  words, with photographs if they have them.
- What they reported stays on the portal, with its state and the appointment
  once staff have agreed one.
- No account, no password: the same link they open their bills with.

## Impact

- Affected specs: `web-tenant-portal`
- Affected code: `frontend/src/features/portal/*`
